Our Services

Managed IT Support

We look after your devices, users and systems day to day. Problems are caught early, updates are applied on schedule, and when something does go wrong your team has a friendly expert to call.

  • Remote and on-site helpdesk
  • Device monitoring and patching
  • User onboarding and offboarding
  • Asset and licence management

Cloud & Microsoft 365

We move your email, files and apps to the cloud safely and set them up properly, with the right licences, permissions and backups, so your team can work securely from anywhere.

  • Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace
  • Email and file migrations
  • Azure and hybrid cloud
  • Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive setup

Cyber Security

Security doesn't need to be complicated. We assess where you stand, close the biggest gaps first, and help you build sensible protection that fits the size of your business.

  • Essential Eight assessments
  • Multi-factor authentication and identity
  • Endpoint protection and email filtering
  • Staff phishing awareness

Network & Infrastructure

From a new office fit-out to replacing ageing equipment, we design and install networks that are fast, secure and easy to support, and we document everything so you're never locked in.

  • Business Wi-Fi and cabling coordination
  • Firewalls and secure remote access
  • Server and NAS deployment
  • Network documentation

Backup & Disaster Recovery

We set up automated, monitored backups of your critical data and cloud services, and we regularly test restores, so you know your business can recover quickly.

  • Microsoft 365 and server backup
  • Off-site and immutable copies
  • Recovery planning and testing
  • Business continuity advice

IT Consulting & Strategy

We take time to understand your business, then give you a clear, prioritised plan. Whether you're growing, relocating or modernising, we manage the project from start to finish.

  • IT health checks and audits
  • Technology roadmaps and budgeting
  • Vendor and procurement advice
  • Project management

AI Solutions Development

Practical AI that saves time and money — chatbots, document automation and smart assistants built safely around your business data, without the hype.

  • AI chatbots & virtual assistants
  • Document & email automation with AI
  • Microsoft Copilot setup & training
  • Safe, private AI for your business data

Cloud & App Development

Modern websites, web apps and cloud software — designed, built and hosted for you, so your business can work faster and serve customers better online.

  • Business websites & web apps
  • Azure cloud apps & APIs
  • Database design & integrations
  • Hosting, maintenance & updates

Automation

Remove boring repetitive work — we connect your apps and automate approvals, reminders, reports and data entry so your team can focus on real work.

  • Power Automate workflows
  • CRM, email & invoice automation
  • Approvals & reminder flows
  • Reports & dashboards on autopilot
