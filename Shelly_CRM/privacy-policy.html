OZFIRSTCHOICE (ABN [insert ABN]) ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and disclose personal information, consistent with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. What information we collect

We may collect:

  • your name, company, email address and phone number;
  • details you provide in enquiries or during the course of providing our services;
  • technical information such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited on our website.

2. How we collect it

We collect information when you complete our contact form, email or call us, engage our services, or browse our website. Our contact form is processed by a third-party form service (Web3Forms), which forwards your enquiry to us by email.

3. Why we use it

  • to respond to your enquiries and provide our services;
  • to manage our relationship with you, including invoicing and support;
  • to improve our website and services;
  • to meet our legal obligations.

We will not sell your personal information. We will only send you marketing communications if you have agreed, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Disclosure

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us run our business (for example, email, hosting and cloud software providers) and only for the purposes above. Some of these providers may store data outside Australia. We take reasonable steps to ensure they protect your information.

5. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Google Search Console) to understand how visitors use the site. This information is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and secure cloud services.

7. Access and correction

You may request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below.

8. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au.
