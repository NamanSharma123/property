About Us

Local IT experts who treat your business like our own.

OZFIRSTCHOICE is a small Australian IT consulting and services company. For more than ten years, our team has helped businesses of all kinds, including professional services, trades, healthcare, retail and not-for-profits, plan, build and support the technology they depend on.

We're deliberately small. That means you deal directly with experienced consultants rather than a call centre, and every client gets the same care and attention whether they have five staff or fifty. Our goal is to be your first choice for IT — the partner you call first because you know we'll answer, understand and fix it.

Frequently asked questions

Do you lock clients into long contracts?

No. Our managed services run month to month. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you in.

Can you work with our existing IT provider or systems?

Yes. We can support your in-house team, take over from a previous provider, or handle specific projects only.

Do you offer on-site support?

Most issues are resolved remotely within minutes, and we're happy to come on site when needed.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your number of users and needs. After a free initial chat we'll give you a clear, fixed quote with no hidden fees.
